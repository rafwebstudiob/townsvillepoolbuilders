Pool costs in Townsville: what affects the price

In short: We don't publish our own pool prices. A local builder has to quote your site. Below are the factors that usually change a quote, and figures from public industry guides, with links.

Use the ranges only as a starting point. What's included in “installed” varies, and fencing, extra earthworks and landscaping are often on top.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We don't set or advertise our own pool prices. The figures below come from published industry guides, with links. They are indicative only, often exclude fencing, excavation extras or landscaping, and are not a quote for a Townsville site. A local builder has to price your block.

Published industry ranges

These are national or Queensland guides, not Townsville-specific quotes:

  • Poolpages' pool installation cost guide (updated November 2026) says about A$45,000–85,000 installed for fibreglass and A$55,000–110,000 or more for concrete, before landscaping.
  • What's The Damage's fibreglass vs concrete guide gives similar installed bands: fibreglass typically about A$50,000–85,000, concrete about A$55,000–110,000, and premium concrete with spa and heating A$120,000–220,000, GST included.
  • Summertime Pools' 2026 fibreglass guide puts a 6–7 m family pool in Queensland at about A$53,000–68,000 installed, before surrounds.

Guides disagree, and what is included in “installed” varies. Use them only as a starting point, then get a written quote for your site.

What usually changes the quote

Type and size

Concrete, fibreglass or plunge; the length, width and depth; and extras such as a spa, waterline tiling, heating, lighting or a cleaner.

The site

Slope, rock, soil type, water table, drainage and how easily a machine can reach the yard can add earthworks, retaining, engineering or a longer install. Hillside and valley streets in Townsville are more likely to need this conversation than a flat suburban lot.

Access

Narrow side access, overhead lines, retaining walls or a shell that has to be craned over the house all affect labour and equipment.

Fencing, paving and landscaping

A compliant pool barrier is required in Queensland. Paving, planting, outdoor rooms and lighting are often quoted separately from the shell. Confirm in writing what is in and out of the price, as the QBCC's “Build a pool” guidance recommends.

Approvals and the North Queensland season

Building approval, inspections and, where needed, engineering are part of a new pool. The wet season can also slow excavation and construction. Allow for timing, not only the dollar figure.

How to use the budget field on our form

The budget bands on the quote form are for you to indicate a range, if you have one. They are not a price list and not a promise that a pool can be built in that band on your site.
