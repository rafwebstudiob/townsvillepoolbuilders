Pool builders in Mount Louisa, Townsville

In short: Request a free pool building quote in Mount Louisa, on Townsville’s western side.

Mount Louisa is a western suburb of Townsville, named for the hill it sits on. Housing is a mix of styles, and some blocks are elevated or sloping, with views towards the city or the coast.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Building a pool in Mount Louisa

Sloping or stepped sites are more common here than on the river flats. That can mean extra earthworks, retaining or a custom shape, and the barrier must still be at least 1,200 mm high at every point, including on a slope, under Queensland’s pool safety standard. Mention slope and access on the form so the builder can allow for it in the quote.

What to mention on your quote request

Note whether the yard falls away, how vehicles would reach the back of the block, and the pool type you have in mind. We pass the enquiry to one local pool builder who services Mount Louisa. There is no fee to enquire and no obligation to book.
